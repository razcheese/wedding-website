A Few Helpful Details

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the dress code?

Our wedding will be cocktail attire. For a June evening in Menlo Park, we suggest mid-length or longer dresses, elegant separates, sport coats with trousers, or suits.

Will the celebration be indoors or outdoors?

Both the ceremony and reception will take place outdoors.

When should I arrive for the ceremony?

Please arrive between 4:00 and 4:15 p.m. The ceremony will begin promptly at 4:30 p.m. To preserve the moment, late arrivals will be asked to wait until the ceremony has concluded.

What is the weather typically like?

June in Menlo Park is typically sunny, with temperatures in the 70s. Our ceremony and reception are planned outdoors, weather permitting, and umbrellas will provide shade during the reception. We recommend bringing a light layer for the evening.

How should I get to the venue?

Complimentary parking is available at the venue, with ample free street parking nearby. Please note that the venue will close when our celebration ends and will not reopen until Monday morning; any car left at the venue cannot be retrieved before then. If you plan to join us for the after-party, we recommend arriving by Uber or Lyft. Transportation to the after-party will depart from the venue at 9:00 p.m.

May I take photos with my phone?

We will be having an unplugged ceremony. Please silence your phone and refrain from taking photos during the ceremony. We look forward to seeing your photos throughout the rest of the celebration.

Will there be a hotel block?

Yes. Please visit the Travel page for our room block and additional nearby accommodations.

May I bring a guest? Are children invited?

As our celebration is limited in size, we are able to accommodate only the guests and children named on each invitation. Thank you for your understanding.
